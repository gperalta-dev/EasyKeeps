This page checks if your phone is telling websites to switch off moving parts.

1. Your phone's Reduce Motion setting

Checking...

Most websites, including Easykeeps, stay still when this is on. To turn it off on an iPhone:

  1. Open Settings
  2. Tap Accessibility
  3. Tap Motion
  4. Turn off Reduce Motion

Then come back to this page. It updates by itself.
