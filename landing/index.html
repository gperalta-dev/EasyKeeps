Questions owners ask

Still not sure? We're happy to talk it through.

Talk to us
Do I need to be good with computers?

No. If you can take a photo with your phone and forward an email, you can do this. We set up everything else for you.

What do I have to do each month?

Snap your receipts or forward your bills to one email address, ideally by the 5th. Your bank feed does the rest, and your report arrives by the 20th.

Is a real person looking at my books?

Yes. Software does the first pass, then our review lead checks every flagged item and approves your month before anything goes to you or the CRA.

Are you accountants? Can you do my taxes?

We're a bookkeeping service run by an accounting student, not a CPA firm, so we don't give tax advice or file tax returns. We keep your books clean and your GST tracked, so your CPA has an easy job at year-end.

Is my financial information safe?

We only use business-grade tools like QuickBooks Online or Xero, with two-step sign-in. We tell you where AI is part of the process, and every change is logged so any number can be traced.

My books are a mess. Can you still help?

Yes. We start with a paid check of where things stand, then give you a cleanup quote before your monthly bookkeeping begins.
